We are

Athena

Aerospace

Nov

2002

A new vision.

Charlie Thomas and Cooper Oldman meet at a NASA symposium in Houston, Texas. Frustrated by the bureaucratic constraints limiting deep space ambitions, the pair spend the following months exchanging ideas about what a privately funded aerospace company could achieve.

FEB

2008

Strong Foundations.

With a shared vision established, Thomas and Oldman begin seeking investment. A chance introduction brings Freya Louise into the fold. Athena Aerospace is formally incorporated in London, England, with an initial seed fund of £40 million.

APR

2015

Ready to Launch.

Athena achieves its first major milestone, successfully conducting a controlled test launch of its proprietary Helios-1 rocket engine from a facility in the Mojave Desert.

Oct

2022

A new Frontier.

Athena announces Project Luna, an ambitious long term programme to establish a permanent human research base on the lunar surface by 2035. The announcement positions Athena as a serious player in the new space race alongside established names.

Our Founders

Charlie Thomas

SpaceX | NASA | ESA

A former NASA mission architect with 15 years experience designing deep space exploration programmes.

Disillusioned by the slow pace of government led space exploration, Charlie left to pursue a more ambitious vision — one where humanity's expansion beyond Earth wasn't bound by political timelines or budget cycles.

Cooper Oldman

SpaceX | Blue Origin

Ex-SpaceX propulsion engineer who led development of several next generation rocket engines. Cooper's obsession with sustainable space travel drove him to seek out like minded individuals.

His belief that lunar infrastructure is the critical first step to interplanetary colonisation became the founding principle of Athena's engineering philosophy.

Freya Louise

Goldman Sachs | NYSE

A decorated aerospace financier and former ESA strategic advisor, Freya brings the business acumen that turns bold ideas into funded realities.

Having secured over £2 billion in public and private investment across her career, she ensures Athena operates with the financial discipline its ambitions demand.

Athena Aerospace Mission statement and background

Our Mission Statement

We're striving to further the frontiers of human exploration by developing sustainable, cost-effective space technologies that enable humanity to thrive beyond Earth.

If it wasn't hard, why would we even try? I mean, what would be the point if we knew we could do it? The fun part is the unknown.
- 2017 Ted Talk Texas, Charlie Thomas

Our founder, Charlie Thomas, made his first steps into the Aerospace industry as an apprentice engineer at just 15 years old.

After nearly 2 decades in the public sector, Charlie decided to use his accumulated contacts and influence to push the Aerospace industry into it's next technological age.
