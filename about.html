We are

Athena

Nov

2002

A new vision.

Charlie Thomas and Cooper Oldman meet at a NASA symposium in Houston, Texas. Frustrated by the bureaucratic constraints limiting deep space ambitions, the pair spend the following months exchanging ideas about what a privately funded aerospace company could achieve. Over late nights and countless whiteboards, the foundational philosophy of Athena begins to take shape.

FEB

2008

Strong Foundations.

With a shared vision established, Thomas and Oldman begin seeking investment. A chance introduction through a mutual contact brings Freya Louise into the fold. Immediately recognising the commercial potential, Freya commits to securing the funding needed to turn ambition into reality. Athena Aerospace is formally incorporated in London, England, with an initial seed fund of £40 million.

APR

2015

Reaching upwards.

Athena achieves its first major milestone, successfully conducting a controlled test launch of its proprietary Helios-1 rocket engine from a facility in the Mojave Desert. The test validates years of engineering work and attracts significant media attention, drawing comparisons to the early days of SpaceX. A Series B funding round raises a further £180 million to accelerate development.

Oct

2022

A new Frontier.

Athena announces Project Luna, an ambitious long term programme to establish a permanent human research base on the lunar surface by 2035. The announcement positions Athena as a serious player in the new space race alongside established names. International partnerships are confirmed with three European space agencies, marking a turning point in the company's global ambitions.

Our Founders

Charlie Thomas

Charlie Thomas A former NASA mission architect with 15 years experience designing deep space exploration programmes. Disillusioned by the slow pace of government led space exploration, Charlie left to pursue a more ambitious vision — one where humanity's expansion beyond Earth wasn't bound by political timelines or budget cycles.

Cooper Oldmaan

Ex-SpaceX propulsion engineer who led development of several next generation rocket engines. Cooper's obsession with sustainable space travel drove him to seek out like minded individuals. His belief that lunar infrastructure is the critical first step to interplanetary colonisation became the founding principle of Athena's engineering philosophy.

Freya Louise

A decorated aerospace financier and former ESA strategic advisor, Freya brings the business acumen that turns bold ideas into funded realities. Having secured over £2 billion in public and private investment across her career, she ensures Athena operates with the financial discipline its ambitions demand.

Our founder, Charlie Thomas, made his first steps into the Aerospace industry as an apprentice engineer at just 15 years old.
