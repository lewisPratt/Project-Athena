Our History

Meeting the challenge set in 1961.

When Kennedy set the goal of reaching the moon by the end of the decade, many thought his confidence was misguided; man was not meant for the stars.

We've decided to take up the challenge and push forwards to mankinds next frontier.

Athena Gallery

Taking the next leap, for all mankind.

We plan to have a permanent base on the moon by 2028.

Construction on the Athena-1 Base camp started in 2024 and we currently have 34 lunar engineers housed on the moon full time.
