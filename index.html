Athena Gallery

Taking the next leap, for all mankind.

We plan to have a permanent base on the moon by 2028.

Construction on the Athena-1 Base camp started in 2024 and we currently have 34 lunar engineers housed on the moon full time.

Current Construction Projects

  1. The main habitation module consists of living quarters, sanitation facilities, social spaces and exercise areas.
  2. Mission control is housed in Coopers Perch, the central administration building that will coordinate all launches from the moons surface.
  3. "The Suburbs", as our engineers have named it, is the outer collection of hab units that will, in future, house the families of our committed "Frontier" team. This team will be the first to commit to living full time on the moon for the foreseeable future.

Lunar Habitation Phase-1

Athena-1 Lunar Base

Athena-1 is our first permanent lunar base, designed to support long-term human presence on the moon. The base has completed phase one of contruction and has been supporting 15 full time resident engineers.

Lunar Sustainability Phase-1

Athena Crucible

The Athena Crucible is our innovative facility designed to support sustainable resource utilization on the moon. It serves as a hub for research and development of technologies that will enable long-term lunar habitation.

  1. The Athena Crucible is our future mining facility that will help to sustain industry on the moon. Plans include resource export, sending rare minerals back to Earth.
  2. Initial construction has been completed on the administration infrastructure surrounding the facility. These buildings will house geologists and astro-mineralists to help discover what's hidden under the moons surface
  3. The transport loop will allow easy transfer of materials between Athena Base and the Crucible, effectively creating a roundabout for haulers to easily navigate.
